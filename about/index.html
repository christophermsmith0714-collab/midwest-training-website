About us

We know facilities.
We know compliance.

Since 1996, Midwest Training & Consulting Services has helped facilities across the country manage environmental compliance without making it more complicated than it needs to be.

We work with the people responsible for keeping facilities running — helping with SPCC, stormwater, tank inspections, reporting, training, and ongoing compliance.

How we work

You work directly with us.

We’re not a call center, and we’re not handing your project off to someone who has never seen your facility.

We take the time to understand your operation, your equipment, and your compliance requirements so the work we provide actually fits your site.

Whether you need one plan, one inspection, or support across multiple facilities, you have a team you can call when something changes or a question comes up.
