Phase I site assessments

Make your next property decision with better information.

Discuss a Phase I Environmental Site Assessment for a property purchase, financing, or another transaction where environmental due diligence matters.

Discuss your project

A good time to get in touch.

  • You are buying or financing a commercial or industrial property.
  • A lender or transaction team has requested environmental due diligence.
  • You need to understand assessment scope and timing before moving forward.

How we can help.

  • Discussion of the property, transaction, and assessment scope
  • Review of relevant historical and environmental records
  • Site reconnaissance and interviews as part of the assessment
  • A report documenting the assessment and findings

Have these details handy.

The property address, transaction timeline, available property records, and any lender or buyer requirements.

Before we get started

Common questions.

When should I get in touch?

Contact us as early as possible in the transaction so the property, required scope, access, and timeline can be discussed.

What should I send about the property?

Start with its address, current use, and transaction deadline. Share any existing environmental reports you have.

Can you discuss a lender’s requirements?

Yes. Include the requirements in your inquiry so the requested scope can be reviewed before work begins.
