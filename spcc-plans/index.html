What is an SPCC Plan?

An SPCC Plan, or Spill Prevention, Control, and Countermeasure Plan, is designed to help facilities prevent oil spills and reduce the risk of oil reaching nearby waterways or shorelines.

The EPA’s SPCC rule may apply to facilities that store, use, or handle oil and have more than 1,320 gallons of aboveground oil storage capacity, depending on the facility and its potential to discharge oil to surface waters.

When you may need an SPCC Plan

  • You are opening or acquiring a facility with oil or fuel storage.
  • Your facility has more than 1,320 gallons of aboveground oil storage capacity.
  • Your tanks, equipment, piping, containment, or operations have changed.
  • You need a new SPCC Plan or an update to an existing plan.
  • Your existing SPCC Plan is approaching its required five-year review.

5-year SPCC Plan reviews

SPCC Plans must be reviewed at least once every five years.

The review helps confirm that the plan still reflects current facility conditions, oil storage, equipment, operating procedures, and spill prevention practices.

If changes are needed to improve spill prevention or control, the SPCC Plan should be updated accordingly.
